We introduce OPAL-Bench, an orientation-invariant, polymorph-aware benchmark for crystal structure prediction (CSP). Instead of matching each prediction to a single reference under fixed tolerances, it aligns the prediction to all known polymorphs and scores it with threshold-free geometric and physical metrics.

How OPAL-Bench works

OPAL-Bench and previous evaluation

Generative CSP methods are usually evaluated with the standard protocol of Match Rate and RMSE. CSPBench, from the crystallographic community, scores traditional methods with point-set and physical distances. OPAL-Bench brings the strengths of both to deep generative models.

Key findings

No method dominates

Rankings change with the metric and the dataset, so a single score cannot tell which method is best.

Geometry is not physics

Geometric and physical distances are only weakly correlated, and they capture complementary aspects of a prediction.

Geometry and physics can coexist

Our baseline, OPAL-CSP, is competitive on geometric metrics and among the best on physical ones, showing that one model can do well on both.
